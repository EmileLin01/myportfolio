# Writing a tabletop role-playing game content with LaTex

This project is a compendium of homebrewed _Dungeons & Dragons_ weapons, armor, and other wondrous items inspired by the equipment of selected _League of Legends_ characters. Each entry includes both flavor text and clearly defined 5e-based gameplay mechanics.

The document was authored and typeset entirely in LaTeX, allowing precise control over layout, presentation, and consistency across entries.

## What this project demonstrates

- Ability to document rules-based content with clear structure and repeatable patterns
- Proficiency with LaTeX for producing polished, print-ready documents

## Assumptions

- The content is evaluated as a documentation and presentation exercise.
- The reader has basic familiarity with Dungeons & Dragons 5e rules.

## Scope

This project focuses on item descriptions, flavor text, and mechanical definitions. Playtesting, balance considerations, and campaign integration are intentionally out of scope.
