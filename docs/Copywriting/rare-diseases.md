# Writing a byline on rare diseases

To keep my copywriting skills current, I occasionally take on small freelance engagements when time allows. In this instance, I wrote a press release for a U.S.-based non-profit organization aimed at raising awareness of rare diseases. The article was subsequently picked up by a media outlet.

This sample demonstrates my ability to work with academic and scientific sources, interpret research findings, extract relevant data, and translate complex information into clear, accessible, and persuasive copy for a general audience.

## What this project demonstrates

- Ability to research and synthesize academic and scientific literature
- Judgment in selecting relevant statistics and framing them for a non-technical audience
- Experience writing persuasive copy suitable for public-facing media

## Assumptions

- Readers have limited prior knowledge of rare diseases or related policy issues.
- The primary goal is awareness and clarity rather than technical depth.  

## Scope

This project focuses on a single press-style article.
