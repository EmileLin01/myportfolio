# Writing a thought leadership article on solar energy

CPCS, a global infrastructure consulting firm, developed a strategy to address Mozambique’s energy constraints through solar power combined with battery storage. I was engaged to ghostwrite a thought leadership article explaining this approach and the associated energy procurement strategy. The article was later published by _Energy Global_, a leading magazine in electrical engineering, and received wide readership.

This sample highlights my ability to work closely with subject-matter experts, ask effective questions, and transform technical concepts into an engaging and accessible narrative suitable for the general public.

## What this project demonstrates

- Ability to obtain and synthesize expert knowledge through interviews
- Editorial judgment in balancing technical accuracy with readability
- Narrative framing for thought leadership and industry publications

## Assumptions

- The target audience includes industry professionals and informed non-specialists
- Readers may have limited familiarity with Mozambique’s energy infrastructure

## Scope

The article is intended to position expertise rather than provide implementation guidance. Engineering specifications, financial modeling, and policy analysis are out of scope.
