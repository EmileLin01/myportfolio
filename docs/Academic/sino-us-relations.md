# Writing a peer-reviewed paper on U.S. politics

This essay was written in 2015 in partial fulfillment of my Master’s degree and was subsequently published in the _Journal of Military and Strategic Studies_, where it received the award for best essay in the volume.

The paper analyzes the U.S. “pivot” toward the Asia-Pacific and evaluates its effectiveness as a hedging strategy in managing China’s rising power. It argues that the pivot remains (at that time) a viable and stabilizing strategy as U.S. and Chinese interests in the region do (did) not fundamentally conflict.

## What this project demonstrates

- Ability to synthesize dense theoretical and empirical literature
- Clear, structured argumentation over long-form analytical writing

## Assumptions

- The reader has baseline familiarity with international relations theory and security studies.
- Strategic behavior is analyzed within historical context.

## Scope

This article focuses on U.S.–China relations in the Asia-Pacific during the Obama administration. Post-2015 developments are out of scope.
