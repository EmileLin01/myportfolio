# Writing a peer-reviewed paper on American foreign policy

This article examines the tension between U.S. grand strategy and public support, using the 2016 presidential transition as a focal point. It analyzes whether a turn toward isolationism and nationalism meaningfully alters America’s long-standing strategic behavior, given its tradition of global engagement and liberal interventionism.

Rather than treating grand strategy as elite-driven alone, the paper highlights public opinion as a constraining factor, discussing likely strategic outcomes when the public is unwilling to bear the costs of hegemonic leadership.

## What this project demonstrates

- Ability to analyze complex political and strategic concepts with nuance
- Academic research, synthesis, and formal argumentative writing

## Assumptions

- The reader has baseline familiarity with international relations or political science concepts.
- The article is evaluated for analytical rigor rather than policy prescription.

## Scope

This paper analyzes U.S. grand strategy and public attitudes during a specific political moment.
