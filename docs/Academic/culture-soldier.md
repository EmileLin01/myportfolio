# Writing a book chapter on American military culture and identities

I co-authored a book chapter called _Schoolhouses and COCOMs: Educating and Cultivating the American-Led International Fraternity of the Uniform_, appearing in _Culture and the Soldier: Identities, Values, and Norms in Military Engagements_, published by UBC Press.

My chapter analyzes how professional military education institutions and combatant commands (COCOMs) reinforce norms and identities within the American-led military order. It explores this transmission of culture elevates interoperability among allied forces beyond formal doctrine.

## What this project demonstrates

- Ability to contribute original analysis to a peer-reviewed, multi-author volume
- Comfort working within conceptual frameworks and editorial constraints
- Ability translating abstract concepts into concrete terms

## Assumptions

- The reader has baseline familiarity with military institutions or security studies.
- Culture is treated as an analytical tool rather than a normative argument.

## Scope

This chapter examines how professional military education and command structures have helped shape military norms and identities in the 21st century. Policy prescriptions are outside the scope of the contribution.
