# Writing a video game guide using Adobe XD

This project is an incomplete gameplay guide for playing Jarvan IV as an ADC in _League of Legends_, created in Adobe XD. While the topic is niche, the goal is to showcase my ability to effectively design and present instructional content.

The emphasis is on layout and presentation rather than on the game itself. Readers unfamiliar with _League of Legends_ should still be able to follow the structure and evaluate the design choices.

## What this project demonstrates

- Ability to design instructional content using a UX and prototyping tool
- Separation of concepts, logic, and contextual explanations 
- Attention to layout, presentation, and readability

## Assumptions

- The reader is familiar with _League of Legends_; understanding of gameplay concepts are assumed. 
- The guide is evaluated primarily as a presentation and design exercise.

## Scope

Explanation of core gameplay concepts and mechanics are intentionally left out of scope.
